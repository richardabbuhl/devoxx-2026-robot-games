## How it is judged
100 POINTS
A panel of Devoxx judges plays every entry and scores it out of 100. Originality carries the most weight — we would rather play one surprising idea than another competent clone — but a clever idea nobody can actually play will not win either.

1. 40
### Originality
Show us something we have not seen. An unexpected genre, mechanic or reading of the brief beats a polished copy of a game that already exists. This is the largest single score, so spend your ideas here.
2. 20
### Realism
Do the robots move like machines with mass, and does the space behave like a building? Weight, momentum, contact with the floor, light that belongs in a cinema.
3. 15
### Playability
A clear goal, controls that explain themselves, honest feedback, and a reason to start a second round.
4. 10
### All three robots
Voxxy, Droid and Biggy each do something only they can do. Three reskins of the same character score low here.
5. 10
### Sense of place
We should know it is the Kinepolis before anyone tells us.
6. 5
### GenAI craft
How you directed the tools, documented in the repo — prompts, iterations, and what you fixed by hand when the model got it wrong.
