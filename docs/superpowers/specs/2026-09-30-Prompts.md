Detailed Design

Layout: Build both floors in one shared coordinate system, using the supplied exhibition and auditorium plans as placement guides. The exhibition hall is at ground level; the auditoriums and their connecting corridors are directly above. A broad grand staircase rises from the exhibition hall and lands in the first-floor foyer. Include the visible entrances, room connections, and seating rows rather than rendering the plans as textures.
Movement and collisions: Use a third-person camera following the selected robot. Wall segments leave real openings at doors and corridors; seating blocks preserve aisles. A smooth walkable ramp beneath the visible staircase lets robots climb between floors. Switching robots retains their distinct movement speeds.
Game flow: Keep the timed three-robot mission, with objectives placed across both levels. Completing it unlocks free exploration. I’ll tune the timer against the actual traversal distances so the added floor and stairs remain playable.
Assumptions: Use 1 scene unit per metre, a 4.5 m floor-to-floor height, roughly 3.6 m ceilings, and a 4 m-wide staircase with visible steps over a smooth collision ramp. Use terrazzo in public areas, carpet and acoustic finishes in auditoriums, and stone/concrete with metal or wood handrails on the stairs. These are consistent working estimates, not surveyed dimensions.
Validation: Check that the scene renders at desktop and mobile sizes, robots cannot pass through walls or seat banks, doorways and aisles remain traversable, and the staircase connects both floors.
